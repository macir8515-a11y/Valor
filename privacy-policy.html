Privacy Policy

VALOR Luxury Restoration, Limassol, Cyprus. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data VALOR (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit our website or contact us about the restoration of a bag or shoes, how we use it, and what rights you have.

Who we are

VALOR is a studio for the restoration of luxury bags and shoes at Agora Anexartisias, Shop 39, Limassol, Cyprus. You can contact us about this policy on WhatsApp at +357 95 985100 or on Instagram at @valor.restore.

What data we collect

  • Contact details you share with us, such as your name, phone number and messaging account.
  • Details of your order: the item, its condition, brand, the services you request, and photos or videos you send us.
  • The content of your messages to us on WhatsApp, Instagram or by phone.

Our website has no accounts, no payment forms and no tracking or advertising cookies.

How we use your data

  • To answer your questions and assess your item.
  • To agree the price and timeline, carry out the work and arrange the return of your item.
  • To keep a record of the condition of your item on arrival and on return.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Legal basis

We process your data to take steps at your request before an order and to carry out the service you ask for (performance of a contract), to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in keeping records of the work we do. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos of your item are used to assess and document the work. We may share images of finished work on our social media only if they do not identify you, and we will ask for your permission first where an item or your details could be recognised.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the services needed to communicate with you and to run this website, and only as far as necessary:

  • WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta), when you message us. Your messages are handled under their terms and privacy policies.
  • Google, which provides the fonts used on this website and the Google Maps link to our studio. Loading them may transmit your IP address to Google.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep data

We keep order records and messages only as long as needed to complete your order, handle any questions about the work, and meet legal requirements. After that we delete them or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, contact us using the details above.

Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you may complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
